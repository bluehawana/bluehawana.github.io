Who Gives the Truck Its Day

Three chairs run every shift. One system has to serve all three.

Demands come from the cab, the fleet office and the customer's goods-in desk — and the orders come from the customer. A cockpit assistant hears only one of them.

The driver's chair
“Break in 15 minutes — where, and can I fuel there?” “There's a tyre symbol on my dashboard. Is it safe to drive on?”
Rest planning, fuel, warning lamps, the handbook — answered in one breath, eyes on the road.
The fleet manager's chair
“XKN 442 — winter tyres today without losing a day?”
The Volvo Truck Center on that route, a slot during the customer stop, the set from the depot tyre hotel — booked, driver told.
The customer's chair
“Change of plan: unload at our storage yard, Radiatorvägen 7. Dock C, crew leaves at 17:00.”
“New e-mail from your customer — read it out?” Read, applied, confirmed back, re-routed. Today that is a phone call to a driver who is driving. Where the orders come from.

Three Agents · One Plate · One Truth

Not one assistant. Three agents that work together on the same truck record.

Co-Driver · in the cab
Voice in, voice out. Knows the plate, the EU driving clock, the service book, the warning lamps. Proactive: speaks before the driver asks — break, fuel, arrival. 33 tools, official APIs only.
ClaimBot · at the service desk
Customers, dealers, invoicers. Plate first, then the contract tier and what it covers, warranty, open campaigns. Drafts the claim (complaint · cause · correction), VST standard times, reject codes — never invented.
Fleet Manager · in the office
Every truck live: position, rest compliance, range, alerts, tyres, faults. Exceptions first. Talks to any driver, books any workshop — one line over the radio, plate first.

The shared record, keyed by the registration plate: VIN · model · service book · contract and warranty · tyres and winter set · driving-time clock · telemetry and faults · dispatch log. All three read it, all three write it — so they can hand off to each other instead of starting over.

One Shift · Three Agents · The Live Demo

A tyre lamp on the E20. Watch the hand-offs.

1 · DRIVER

“What does that symbol mean? Is it safe?”

Co-Driver reads the sensors and the manual page TPMS-11: rear left 7.4 bar, not dropping — safe to the stop. Then the reset steps.

2 · CO-DRIVER

Lamp stays on → tyre service

Booked at Volvo Truck Center Örebro, 14:30, during unloading. “Keep under 80, check pressure at every stop.”

3 · CLAIMBOT

Who pays?

Same record: contract tier, coverage, campaign. Covered — the workshop sees it before the truck arrives; the claim is drafted, not argued.

4 · FLEET MANAGER

“Winter tyres today, no lost day?”

Joins the same slot. Set from the depot tyre hotel on the 11:00 shuttle. Studded — legal from 1 Oct. Driver told in one line.

5 · CUSTOMER

“New e-mail — read it out?”

“Yes.” New unloading address, dock C, crew leaves 17:00 — read in one breath, applied, confirmed back. “Update the route” → Maps re-routes. Still on time.

What the driver hears: one sentence each time, under 20 words. What the office sees: the whole chain on one card. What no third-party assistant can do: any of it — it needs the service book, the contract and the fleet.

Architecture · How We Achieve It

Voice in, voice out — why it can answer almost anything.

One path for every request: speech → text → LLM → tools → action → speech. The LLM reasons, the tools do, the cab speaks. Measured end to end: about one second per turn.

1 · HEAR

Speech → text

“Hello Volvo” wakes it. Whisper transcribes — any language, under half a second.

2 · THINK

Text → intent

LLM agent decides what to do. Gemma 4 12B on Ollama; Qwen backup; any LLM.

3 · KNOW

Tools, not guesses

33 tools: the truck record, the service wiki, workshops, Maps, weather, Spotify, markets, web search — live data, official APIs.

4 · ACT

Executes in the cab

Google Maps, Spotify, climate, media — via Android intents and our native bridges. Bookings and e-mails from the cloud.

5 · SPEAK

Text → speech

TTS renders one sentence; the cab plays it. Eyes on the road.

Stack: Android Automotive (AOSP) · Kotlin + WebView · FastAPI · Whisper · Ollama / Gemma 4 · MLX · TTS · official APIs — built with Claude Code.

Tech Summary · How It Was Built

Open source where it counts. Ours where it matters.

Vehicle · the thin client
Android Automotive OS 15 (AOSP) · Kotlin app + WebView UI · ExoPlayer audio · Porcupine on-device wake word · Spotify App Remote SDK · Google Maps intents
Speech in · speech out
Whisper large-v3-turbo for speech-to-text, any language · server-side TTS to WAV (NVIDIA Riva voices) — the cab only records and plays
LLM · the brain
Gemma 4 12B on Ollama — the agent, tool calling, benchmarked against 26B, gpt-oss and Qwen 3.5 · Qwen 2.5 3B on MLX — failover · nomic-embed — knowledge base · OpenAI-compatible: swap to any model
Cloud · three agents, one record
Python FastAPI · Co-Driver agent loop with 33 tools · ClaimBot and Fleet Manager chat agents on the same plate-keyed record · fleet dashboard · official APIs only: Google Maps, Spotify, Open-Meteo, Yahoo Finance, Nominatim/OSRM, DuckDuckGo
Development platform
MacBook Pro M5 Max 128 GB runs the LLMs locally · Android Studio + Automotive emulator · Claude Code as AI pair-programmer · GitHub · Python 3 · Gradle · every demo run logged, every turn timed
Accounts & subscriptions
Spotify Premium + developer app · Google Maps Platform key · TripAdvisor API · Picovoice (wake word) · Claude subscription · everything else open source
