Unconventional Path to Technology

Born in Ürümqi, northern China, my path has been anything but conventional. I spent a decade as a bank director in Tianjin—building deep expertise in international business and cross-cultural collaboration—before moving to Gothenburg in 2016.

This wasn't just a geographical move; it was a total professional reboot. I pursued additional degrees in International Business and Technology, ultimately discovering that code is the ultimate bridge between diverse business challenges and innovative solutions.

Today I work as Full-Stack Developer & AI Solution Engineer at Volvo Group Digital Technology & Operations in Gothenburg, building and running the on-premises AI platform and the AI agents that run on it, after six years across full-stack development and cloud infrastructure. I own what I build end to end in production, including its roadmap, incidents and costs.
