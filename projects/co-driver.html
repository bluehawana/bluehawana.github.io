How it works

One record, three agents

Co-Driver · in the cab

Voice in, voice out. Knows the plate, the EU driving-time clock, the service book and the warning lamps. Speaks before the driver asks: break due, fuel, arrival.

ClaimBot · at the service desk

Plate first, then contract tier, warranty and open campaigns. Says who pays, reads the workshop manual, books the slot, drafts the claim.

Fleet Manager · in the office

Every truck live, exceptions first. The hub: it talks to any driver, asks ClaimBot, and passes the customer's requests on to the cab.
